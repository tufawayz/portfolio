What I do

  1. At Pfizer Italy I take a use case from the idea to a test with the people who will use it. So far: an agent that checks expense documents and flags what doesn’t add up, one that compares supplier proposals for events, and an assistant that helps plan accounts by pulling data from SharePoint.

    I’ve worked on other things too: an agent that reads incoming emails and picks the route (ready-made answer, ticket or a person), an automated compliance check on survey responses, and line-by-line verification of what an agent returns against its source.

    The hard part isn’t getting the model to answer. It’s deciding what it should know, and what it must never do.

How I build an agent

Five steps,always the same.

  1. I

    Listen

    I sit down with the people who do the job and have them walk me through it.

  2. II

    Scope

    I pick the smallest part worth automating.

  3. III

    Instructions and sources

    I write what the agent must do and which documents it can draw on.

  4. IV

    Real cases

    I test it on real cases and fix where it goes wrong.

  5. V

    A person decides

    The agent flags and suggests. The last word isn’t its own.
